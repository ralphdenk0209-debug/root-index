Privacy Policy – Root Index

Last updated: November 2026

This is the English version of our privacy policy. Additional information for users in the United Kingdom, the United States and France is set out in sections 21 to 23. Users in the United States should also read our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

This privacy policy covers the web service at root-index.de and root-index.com. Additional processing by a native smartphone app will be explained separately before it is used.

2. What data we process

Depending on how you use the service, we process in particular:

• Account data: email address, user ID, sign-in and security settings and, where applicable, your name.
• Profile and usage data: for example date of birth, dietary preference, favourites, shopping lists, saved loyalty cards, your own recipes and plans.
• Health-related data: for example food diary, weight, body measurements, training, sleep, steps, supplements, cycle and voluntary information about medical conditions.
• Contributions and requests: uploaded photos, product information, questions to RIKI, contact requests and the related processing results.
• Contract data: selected plan, subscription status, payment and invoice information, and notices of cancellation and withdrawal.
• Technical data: for example IP address, time and target of an access, browser information and error messages.
• Records of consent: content, time and assignment of consents given or withdrawn.

Not every one of these data types is processed on every visit.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data for the following purposes:

• To provide your account and the functions you request and to perform the contract, based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
• Based on your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, where processing is voluntary and requires consent.
• For health data, additionally based on your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.
• To comply with legal obligations, for example tax retention obligations, under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
• For secure provision, troubleshooting and prevention of misuse under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to operate the service reliably and to protect it against unauthorised access.

Which legal basis applies depends on the particular processing operation.

4. User account and minimum age

Root Index is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

For account-based functions we need in particular your email address and technical sign-in data. Without this information we cannot provide a personal account.

Further information is only required if you want to use the function based on it. For example, individual requirement calculations need the body and activity information requested for this purpose.

Consent under data protection law is distinct from agreeing to a paid subscription. For contracts with minors, the statutory requirements and the Terms also apply.

5. Health data and personal analyses

We use health-related information to provide the documentation, calculation and analysis functions you have chosen.

Entering such information or agreeing to the Terms does not replace explicit consent to the processing of health data.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, in particular by email to kontakt@root-index.de or via the function provided in the app (“Withdraw consent”).

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. We then stop the processing based on this consent and delete the data concerned, unless a statutory exception permits further storage.

Functions that need this data will then no longer be available, or only to a limited extent.

Without health consent you can still use in particular: product search and barcode scan, product ratings, favourites, shopping lists, recipes and questions to RIKI without personal health reference.

Deleted immediately upon withdrawal: food diary, weight history, body measurements, supplements, water, sleep and step data, daily condition, training data and training profile, goal history and the age and body information in your profile.

Backups: our database is backed up automatically. These backups are overwritten on a rolling basis and deleted after 30 days at the latest. Until then they are not used, except for restoration after a technical failure; deleted data is not reactivated in that case.

6. Hosting and data storage

INWX (website and email)

Our website and our email mailbox are operated by INWX GmbH, Prinzessinnenstraße 30, 10969 Berlin, Germany, on servers in Germany. When a page is accessed, technical connection data, in particular the IP address and information about the request, are processed.

The processing serves the delivery and security of the website. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We have concluded a data processing agreement with INWX under Art. 28 GDPR.

GitHub (source code and publication)

We manage the website’s program code at GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA). From there it is transferred automatically to our web server at INWX. No visitor data is transmitted to GitHub. The website itself is delivered exclusively via INWX. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Supabase

We use Supabase for sign-in, database, file storage and server-side functions. The account, content and technical data required for each function are processed.

The legal bases depend on the respective purpose; for health data, section 5 also applies.

Our contractual partner is Supabase, Inc., USA, as processor. Our project is located in the EU region (Frankfurt am Main, eu-central-1); the database, files and backups are stored there. The basis is Supabase’s data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. For operations, logs and support, employees and sub-processors of Supabase may also access data from third countries, in particular the USA.

A European database location does not automatically mean that all processing takes place exclusively within the EU.

7. Storage on your device and external program libraries

The app stores information on your device, for example to keep you signed in, for your chosen settings and for the operation of the installable web app.

Where such storage or access is strictly necessary to provide a service you have expressly requested, this is based on § 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG (and equivalent rules such as Art. 82 of the French Data Protection Act and the UK PECR). Any access beyond this that requires consent needs your prior consent.

The subsequent processing of personal data is additionally governed by the GDPR.

Specifically, we store in your browser’s local storage: your sign-in session (until you sign out), display settings such as filters, dark mode or view (until you change them or clear your browser data) and, via the service worker, a copy of the program files for fast loading and offline use (replaced with each new version). We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Some program libraries are loaded via jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): Supabase’s sign-in and database library and the barcode recognition (Quagga2). The delivery infrastructure receives technical connection data, in particular your IP address. jsDelivr uses delivery networks such as Cloudflare and Fastly, including ones based in the USA. We do not store anything in this process. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the fast and reliable provision of the program functions. Section 17 applies to transfers to the USA.

8. Camera, barcode scan and photos

During a barcode scan, the camera image is analysed on your device to recognise the product number. The recognised number is then used for the product search.

This is different from functions where you expressly upload photos for processing, for example photos of labels, products or recipes. These files are transferred to our servers and stored and analysed there to process your request.

Where necessary for the requested function, processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Any further use requires a separate legal basis.

Please do not photograph people, private documents or other personal information that is not needed.

Product information you submit may result in entries in the public product catalogue. Only product details (name, brand, ingredients, nutritional values) and, where applicable, a product image are published. Your name, email address or other account data are never published. We keep original label photos until the product has been reviewed, for 12 months at most; they are then deleted. Photos of pantry items or recipes are used only for the respective request and are not published.

9. RIKI and AI-supported functions

RIKI is an AI assistant. It helps, for example, with reading labels, with recipe functions and with answering questions. Answers are generated by AI and may be inaccurate.

The input required for each request is processed, such as photos, texts, product information and the related app context. Profile or diary information may also be relevant for personalised functions.

The legal basis for processing ordinary personal data required for the requested function is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The processing of health data must additionally be covered by explicit, sufficiently specific consent.

AI provider used: Anthropic PBC, USA (AI models “Claude”). We do not currently use any other AI providers. Per function, the following is transmitted:

• Label and product analysis: photos and texts of labels and product information.
• Questions to RIKI: your question and a page context limited to the question.
• Recipe suggestions: the ingredients, meal and dietary preference you specify and your daily calorie requirement.
• Pantry and recipe photos: the photos you upload.
• Personal weekly recommendation: summarised information about your eating and training habits. This function uses health data and is only available with your health consent.

We do not transmit individual diary entries, weight, body measurements or information about medical conditions to Anthropic; for the weekly recommendation only summarised values are transferred. The transfer to the USA is based on Anthropic’s contractual and data protection terms including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and stores it only for a short period for abuse monitoring, currently no longer than 30 days.

10. Voice input

If you activate voice input, the speech recognition function of your browser or operating system is used. Depending on the system, speech may be transmitted to its provider. The recognised text is then processed like a typed text request.

We use the speech recognition provided by your browser (Web Speech API). In Google Chrome, the recording is transmitted to Google (Google LLC, USA / Google Ireland Ltd.), in Safari to Apple; other browsers may use their own services. Root Index itself does not receive or store any audio data, only the recognised text. Recording only starts when you tap the microphone icon and your browser grants microphone access. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by starting voice input. You can use text input instead at any time.

11. Open Food Facts and external product data

For product search and to complete product information we use, among other sources, Open Food Facts (Open Food Facts Association, France).

If your browser sends a request directly, Open Food Facts receives the product number searched for and technical connection data, in particular your IP address. If the request is made via our server, Open Food Facts generally receives that server’s connection data. Loading external product images directly, in particular from Open Food Facts, also creates connections to the respective image provider.

The processing serves the requested product search and display. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, where the processing is necessary for this purpose.

12. Payments via Stripe

We use Stripe for payments for Root Index Premium. You enter your payment information with Stripe during the payment process.

Depending on the transaction, contact, billing, payment and transaction data are processed for contract and payment processing. We receive or can view the information required for administration, for example customer and subscription IDs, payment status, amounts and invoice information. We do not see or store full card or account details.

The legal bases are Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract processing and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR for legal obligations. Stripe also processes certain data under its own responsibility, for example for fraud prevention and to comply with its own legal obligations.

Our contractual partner is Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland. Stripe may transfer data to Stripe, Inc. in the USA; it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition. The payment methods shown in the checkout are available, for example credit card. More information: Stripe Privacy Policy.

13. Contact, contract messages and newsletter

If you contact us, we process your contact details and the content of your message to handle your request. For contract-related requests this is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, for other requests on our legitimate interest in handling them under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Sign-in, security and contract messages are sent to provide the service or to comply with legal obligations. Our mailbox and the sending of all emails, including sign-in and confirmation emails, run on the servers of INWX GmbH in Germany (processing on our behalf, see section 6). We delete contact requests 12 months after the request has been dealt with, unless there is a retention obligation.

We do not currently send a newsletter. If one is introduced, it will only be sent on the basis of your prior consent confirmed by email; you can withdraw this consent at any time for the future. Open or click tracking does not take place.

14. Push notifications

If you activate push notifications, technical delivery information is processed, in particular a delivery address and related keys. The push service of your browser or operating system provider may be involved in delivery. You can revoke the permission in your browser or device settings.

We currently only send notifications about changes to a shared shopping list and about the upcoming end of your Premium trial. Notifications do not contain health information. Depending on the device, the push services of Google, Mozilla, Microsoft or Apple are involved. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR in conjunction with § 25(1) TDDDG), which you give when activating them. We delete the delivery information as soon as you switch off notifications or delete your account; sent notifications are deleted after 90 days.

15. Visitor statistics

Root Index includes a function for counting page views and for the aggregated attribution of traffic sources, for example search engines or direct visits.

Only totals per day, page type and traffic source are stored. No IP addresses, cookies, device IDs or user IDs are stored; it is not possible to identify individuals. We keep the daily figures for up to 24 months. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is to understand the use of our service in anonymous form. Consent is not required because nothing is stored on or read from your device.

In addition, we count how often individual functions are used, for example product search, barcode scan, viewing a product or recipe, questions to RIKI and opening individual sections. Here too, only a total per day, function and platform (web, iPhone app, Android app) is stored, together with whether the use was signed in or as a guest – without user ID, IP address, cookie or device ID. To leave out accounts of our own team, the sign-in is checked briefly but not stored. Retention and legal basis as above.

In addition, our host INWX stores technical server logs (including IP address, time, requested address) for security and troubleshooting. We do not analyse these for statistical purposes; they are deleted automatically by the host after a short time.

16. No advertising and no affiliate links

Root Index contains no advertising and no affiliate links. Links to external pages, for example manufacturer pages or Google Search, do not load any content before you click; a connection is only established when you click the link. The respective operator then processes data under its own responsibility.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area must meet the requirements of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR. Depending on the recipient, an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply. In detail:

• Open Food Facts Association: France – no third-country transfer.
• Supabase, Inc. (database, sign-in): storage in the EU, access from the USA possible – EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
• Anthropic PBC (AI functions): USA – EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
• Stripe (payments): Ireland and USA – EU-US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
• GitHub, Inc. (source code, no visitor data): USA – EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
• jsDelivr and its delivery networks (program libraries): EU and USA – EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the network operators’ EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
• Push and speech recognition services of Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla: depending on the device, USA – EU-US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Information on the safeguards used and, where applicable, a copy is available on request at kontakt@root-index.de.

18. Retention and deletion

We store personal data only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention obligations. Account-related content is generally stored for as long as you use the respective functions. After deletion or withdrawal, the statutory deletion requirements apply; necessary contract and evidence data are handled separately.

Invoices and other accounting records are generally kept for eight years, certain business letters for six years and certain tax records for ten years (German law). The period generally begins at the end of the calendar year concerned.

• Account and profile: until your account is deleted. Deletion via “Delete account” takes effect immediately.
• Health data: until you withdraw your health consent or delete your account; then immediate deletion (see section 5).
• Label and product photos: until the product has been reviewed, 12 months at most. Pantry and recipe photos: only for the duration of the request.
• AI requests and results: as long as needed for the respective function, at the latest until your account is deleted. For the AI provider see section 9.
• Contact requests: 12 months after the request has been dealt with.
• Technical logs: 90 days at most.
• Records of consent and notices of cancellation and withdrawal: for the duration of the account or contract and then three years from the end of the year (regular limitation period), unless a longer retention obligation applies.
• Backups: rolling, overwritten after 30 days at the latest.
