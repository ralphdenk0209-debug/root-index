Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy applies to consumer health data of users in the United States, in particular under the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada Senate Bill 370 and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. It supplements our general Privacy Policy. Provider: Ralph Denk – Root Index, Auweg 23, 84103 Postau, Germany, kontakt@root-index.de.

1. Categories of consumer health data we collect

• Food diary entries, meals, calories and nutrients;
• weight, body measurements, body composition and goal history;
• training, sleep, steps, water intake and daily condition;
• supplements you record;
• menstrual cycle information, if you choose to enter it;
• information about medical conditions or intolerances that you voluntarily enter;
• age, sex and activity information used to calculate your requirements;
• information derived from the above, for example your calculated energy and nutrient requirements and your personal weekly recommendation.

2. Sources

We collect consumer health data only directly from you (your entries in the app) and derive further information from these entries within the app. We do not buy health data from third parties and we do not use geofencing.

3. Purposes

We collect and use consumer health data exclusively to provide the documentation, calculation and analysis functions you have chosen (for example the food diary, nutrient analysis, requirement calculation, training and weekly recommendation), to keep the service secure and to comply with legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising.

4. Consent

We collect consumer health data only after you have given your separate, explicit opt-in consent in the app. Agreeing to our Terms does not count as consent. You can use the app without this consent (product search, ratings, shopping lists, recipes). You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app via “Withdraw consent” or by email; the data listed in section 5 of our Privacy Policy is then deleted immediately.

5. Sharing

We do not sell consumer health data and we do not share it with third parties or affiliates (we have no affiliates). We only disclose it to the following processors, who act on our behalf under contract and may not use it for their own purposes:

• Supabase, Inc. – hosting of database and sign-in (storage in the EU);
• Anthropic PBC – AI provider, only summarised values for the personal weekly recommendation, not used for model training.

Should we ever intend to share consumer health data with a third party, we would first ask for your separate consent, which would name the recipient and the purpose.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:
• confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data and access this data, including a list of all third parties and affiliates with whom it has been shared (currently: none, only the processors named above);
• withdraw your consent to the collection and sharing of your consumer health data;
• have your consumer health data deleted – we also instruct our processors to delete it.

To exercise your rights, use the functions in the app (“Withdraw consent”, “Delete account”) or write to kontakt@root-index.de. We respond within 45 days; this period may be extended once by 45 days where reasonably necessary, and we will tell you if so. Exercising your rights is free of charge.

7. Appeals

If we decline your request, you can appeal by email to kontakt@root-index.de with the subject “Appeal – health data”. We will respond in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov/file-complaint) or the Attorney General of your state.

8. Changes

We will inform you of material changes to this policy in the app before they take effect. We will ask for new consent before using consumer health data for any additional purpose.
