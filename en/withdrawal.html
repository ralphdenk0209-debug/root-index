You have the right to withdraw from (cancel) this contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period will expire after 14 days from the day of the conclusion of the contract.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we shall reimburse to you all payments received from you without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we are informed about your decision to withdraw from this contract.

We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such reimbursement.

If you requested to begin the performance of services during the withdrawal period, you shall pay us an amount which is in proportion to what has been provided until you have communicated to us your withdrawal from this contract, in comparison with the full coverage of the contract.

The agreed seven-day trial remains free of charge. We do not charge any compensation for this period, even in the event of withdrawal.

Additional information on the trial

The withdrawal period begins when the contract is concluded, not when the first payment is charged. Activating and using Root Index Premium does not in itself cause your right of withdrawal to expire. Cancellation during the free trial and the statutory right of withdrawal exist independently of each other. The term and cancellation of the subscription are governed by the Terms and Conditions.
