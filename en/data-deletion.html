Data deletion

This page explains how you can delete your data at Root Index. It applies to the web app at root-index.de and to the iPhone app “Root Index”.

1. Delete your account and all data

In the app: Menu → “Abo & Konto” (Subscription & account) → “Konto löschen” (Delete account). To confirm, type LÖSCHEN.

This immediately and permanently deletes your diary, profile, goals, body measurements, favourites, own recipes, training, water and supplements, and your account. This cannot be undone.

If you have an active Premium subscription, please cancel it first: in the browser under “Abo & Konto” → “Verträge hier kündigen” (Cancel contracts here), in the iPhone app in the subscription settings of your Apple ID. Invoice data that we must keep for tax reasons remains stored with the payment provider.

2. Delete only your health data

In the app: Menu → “Rechtliches” (Legal) → “Einwilligung widerrufen” (Withdraw consent). All health data is deleted immediately: diary, weight, body measurements, sleep, steps, supplements, cycle and training. Your account remains.

3. Unsubscribe from the newsletter

Every newsletter email contains an unsubscribe link. After that you will not receive any further issues.

4. Deletion request by email or letter

If you can no longer access your account, or want other data deleted (for example uploaded photos or a contact request), write to kontakt@root-index.de or to the postal address in the legal notice. Please state the email address of your account so we can find it. We will confirm the deletion.

How long each type of data is kept is set out in our Privacy Policy (section 18), together with your other rights, such as access.

5. Meta app “Content Studio”

For its own work on Instagram and Facebook, Root Index uses a Meta app called “Content Studio”. It only processes data of Root Index’s own accounts (own posts, their insights and comments on these posts) and public hashtag posts, which are only analysed in aggregate and without usernames. If you want data from this app to be deleted, write to kontakt@root-index.de or to the postal address in the legal notice.
