Two folders side by side on a Mac

To move files from one folder to another you want to see both at once – but Finder has no two-pane view.

How to do it in Finder

With Finder you use two windows:

  1. Open a Finder window and press ⌘N for a second one.
  2. Arrange them side by side. From macOS 15, windows snap into place when you drag them to the left or right edge of the screen.
  3. Drag files from one window to the other. On the same drive they're moved, to a different drive they're copied.

You can also open tabs with ⌘T and drop files onto a tab. That saves space, but you only ever see one folder.

How to do it with Foldair

Foldair shows two folders in one window, like a classic commander:

  1. Click “Two folders” at the top. Each side gets its own folder and address bar.
  2. Press Tab to switch the active side.
  3. F5 copies the selection to the other side, F6 moves it. F7 creates a new folder, F8 moves to the Trash.

“Compare folders” (⇧⌘Y) shows what is only on the left, only on the right, or newer – also between Mac, NAS and server.

Frequently asked questions

Can Finder show two folders in one window?

No. Finder shows one folder per window. You can place two windows side by side or use tabs.

Do F5 and F6 work on every Mac?

On some keyboards the F keys control brightness or volume. Then also press fn (or the Globe key). The commands are also in the “Two folders” menu.
