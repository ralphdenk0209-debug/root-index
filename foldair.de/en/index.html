Frequently asked questions

Is Foldair safe, even though it's not from the App Store?

Yes. Foldair is signed with an Apple developer certificate and checked by Apple for malicious software (notarized). That's why it opens without a warning. Many well-known Mac apps are sold directly the same way. Behind Foldair is a real person with an address in the legal notice.

Does Foldair replace Finder?

Foldair runs alongside Finder. You can keep using Finder and use Foldair for everything Finder is missing.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Rules, duplicate files, photos and receipts work on any Mac running macOS 14. With Apple Intelligence, Foldair gets even better at understanding file names and recipes.

Does Foldair send my files or emails anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your Mac. Foldair only checks this website now and then to see if there's an update.

What happens after the 14 days?

Browsing, copying, moving and renaming folders stays free. The agent, device import and batch renaming then need a license.

How do I get my license?

By email after your purchase. In Foldair, choose “Foldair › License…” and paste it in – done, even without an internet connection.

Can I undo things?

Yes. Everything the agent moves is listed in the history and can be restored. Nothing gets deleted without your confirmation.
