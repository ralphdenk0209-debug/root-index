Description (150 words)

Foldair is a file explorer for macOS, made for people coming from Windows Explorer or struggling with Finder. It runs alongside Finder and adds what's missing there: a folder tree, an address bar you can type paths into, cut and paste with ⌘X and ⌘V, tabs, and two folders side by side with F5 to copy and F6 to move. Folder sizes appear right in the list, and servers connect via SFTP, FTP, FTPS, SMB or WebDAV.

On top of that comes an agent: it tidies up downloads, sorts holiday photos, files invoices from Apple Mail and finds duplicate files. It suggests, the user decides, and everything can be undone. The AI is Apple Intelligence on the user's own Mac – nothing gets uploaded.

Foldair can be tried free for 14 days, after which the explorer stays free. A license costs a one-time €19.99 and up, with no subscription. Developed by Ralph Denk in Bavaria, Germany.
