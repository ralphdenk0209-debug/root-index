2. This website

This website uses no cookies and no advertising services, and it does not load any third-party fonts or scripts. The site is hosted by INWX GmbH, Prinzessinnenstraße 30, 10969 Berlin, on servers in Germany. When you visit it, technically necessary connection data is processed, in particular your IP address, the time, the file requested and browser information. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have a data processing agreement with INWX under Art. 28 GDPR.

Visitor count: so that we know whether the site is being found, it increments a counter when a page is opened and when “Download” or “Buy” is clicked. Only daily totals are stored, together with the rough origin of the visit (e.g. “Google”, “Instagram” or “direct”) – no cookie, no identifier, no IP address and no profile; individual visitors cannot be identified from this. The counting runs at our processor Supabase in a data center in the EU (Frankfurt am Main, Germany). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in simple reach measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. The Foldair app

Foldair processes your files, photos and emails exclusively on your Mac. The AI features use Apple Intelligence on the device. No content is transferred to us or to third parties. There is no user account and no usage tracking.

Ratings: after one day of use, Foldair asks once whether you would like to rate it. Only if you click “Send” are your stars, your text, an optional first name, the Foldair version and the language sent to us – without an identifier, without an IP address in the table, without any files. They are stored at Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt am Main). A rating only appears on foldair.de if you ticked the box allowing it to be shown on foldair.de and we approve it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can withdraw it at any time by emailing kontakt@root-index.de, and we will then delete the rating.

Update check: when it starts and every 6 hours, Foldair fetches the file update.json from this website. This creates the log data described in section 2 at the hosting provider. You can turn the check off in Foldair.

License: the license key contains your name, email address, license type and date. It is stored only on your Mac and verified there without an internet connection.

4. Purchase

Payments are handled by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland (privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy). Stripe processes your payment details; we never see full card or account details. We receive your name, email address and order details in order to generate the license key (via our service provider Supabase, servers in the EU) and to meet statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). If you write to us, we process your details to handle your request and delete them 12 months after it has been resolved, unless we are required to keep them.
